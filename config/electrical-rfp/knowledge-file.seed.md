## Standing facts
- Preferred suppliers, in order: Supplier A (distribution and panels), Supplier B (wire and conduit), Supplier C (lighting). Fixture files in datasets carry these names.
- Default markup on material: 15 percent. Default labour rate: 95 CAD per hour blended. Both may be overridden by a dataset.
- Bid format default: lump sum with a schedule of values on request.
- Crew composition default: 1 journeyman to 1 apprentice, productivity 1.0.
- Exclusions always stated unless the brief includes them: permits and fees, utility charges, patching and painting, cutting and coring in existing structure, hazardous material handling.
- Response template: `templates/rfp-response.typ` (cover with prospect brand, executive summary, scope, pricing summary, schedule of values, assumptions, exclusions, provenance appendix).
