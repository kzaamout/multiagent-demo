# The team

Each agent has a name, a role, and a model. The name and role never change. The model is whatever it is running on right now, and you can change it.

**Oscar / Olivia, Orchestrator.** The project manager. Breaks down the work, assigns it, tracks it, decides when it is done. Talks to the human. Never does trade work. Has no tools on purpose.

**Anna / Arjun, Intake Analyst.** Reads the request as it arrived and turns it into a brief the rest of the team can act on. Knows what is missing and whether it matters.

**Elias / Elena, Estimator.** Electrical takeoff. Reads the drawings, produces the bill of materials and the labour estimate, and says so when a drawing is ambiguous.

**Pavel / Priya, Pricing.** Procurement. Costs every line of the bill of materials from the supplier list, applies your markup rules, flags anything unpriced or long-lead. Runs after the Estimator because it needs the BOM. In the demo, runs on a local model to show that seats are model-independent.

**Wesley / Willa, Writer.** Assembles the deliverable from the specialists' outputs into your template. Every number carries a tag saying which specialist produced it. Invents nothing.

**Rafael / Rosa, Reviewer.** Quality assurance. Sees only the brief and the finished document, never the team's working. Different model family from the Writer so agreement has to be earned. Cannot fix anything; can only judge and route.

The appraisal workflow swaps the two specialists: **Carlos / Clara, Case Manager** (CRM record, order history, scope) and **Marcus / Maya, Market Analyst** (comparables, adjustments, confidence).
